Create a full-screen animated scene of 7 cats jumping in different directions, inside of a living room. The cats should be realistic and have distinct body shapes and colors, with each cat jumping in a unique direction. The scene should be bright and colorful, with a day time atmosphere.

The composition should be clean and as realistic as possible. The cats should be the main focus, with their jumps and movements clearly visible. They usually jump in a straight line, but can also jump diagonally or in a curved motion. When they jump their arms and legs should move with the cat, not just stand still. At least one cat should be jumping straight at the camera, with their arms and legs moving along with their body.

Cats faces should be as accurate as possible, with their eyes, mouths, and facial features clearly visible. They should be jumping in slow motion, not high speed. The animation should loop naturally, run smoothly, adapt to the browser viewport, and keep all cats visible without cropping. Use a full-page canvas or SVG and no external libraries.

Build and edit the file in small chunks instead of writing a monolith in one go, so it's easier for you to modify and debug components if anything doesn't work as expected.
